## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where is my information stored?

Your House Records information is stored locally on your device. The app also supports backups to your personal iCloud Drive storage.

### Does House Records require an account?

No. House Records does not require a separate account or subscription.

### How do I back up my records?

Open House Records and navigate to the backup options in Settings. You can create a manual backup or use the available automatic backup functionality.

### Can I restore my information?

Yes. Use the restore option in House Records to restore data from an available backup.

### Does the developer have access to my records?

No. Your household records are not uploaded to developer-operated servers.
