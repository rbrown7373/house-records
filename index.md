# House Records — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 8, 2026**

House Records is a home inventory and maintenance management application developed by Rick Brown. Your privacy is important. House Records is designed to keep your household information under your control without requiring an account or collecting your personal records on developer-operated servers.

## 1. Information Stored

House Records allows you to organize and store information about your home, including:

- Appliances, electronics, tools, furniture, and household equipment
- Product models, serial numbers, purchase dates, and warranties
- Maintenance schedules and service histories
- Contractors and service provider contact information
- Replacement parts and purchase records
- Photographs, receipts, documents, and scanned product labels

This information is stored locally on your device using Apple's data storage technologies.

The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores your household records.

## 2. iCloud Backup

House Records supports manual and automatic backups using Apple's iCloud Drive service.

When iCloud backup is available and configured, backup files may contain your saved household information, photographs, attachments, and related records.

These backups are stored in your personal iCloud Drive storage, managed through your Apple Account.

The developer does not receive or have access to your iCloud backups.

Information stored through iCloud is subject to Apple's applicable privacy and security practices.

## 3. Camera, Photos, Files, and Contacts

House Records may request access to your device's camera to photograph household items, capture documents, and scan product labels.

You may also select photographs and files to attach to your records.

The app allows you to select individual contacts when adding contractors or service providers. Only the contact information you choose to import is saved to your household records.

These features are used to provide the functionality you request. Information accessed through these features is not transmitted to the developer.

## 4. Data Collection and Tracking

House Records does not collect your personal household records for the developer.

The application does not include:

- Third-party advertising
- Advertising trackers
- Developer-operated analytics services
- Tracking across other companies' apps or websites
- A requirement to create a House Records account

The developer does not sell your personal information or share your household records with data brokers or advertisers.

## 5. External Links and Services

House Records may allow you to open product websites, purchase links, maps, telephone numbers, or email applications.

When you choose to use these features, external services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

House Records does not control the privacy practices of external websites or services.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your household records remain on your device until you modify or delete them or remove the app's associated local data.

You may edit or delete individual records within House Records.

Deleting House Records and its local data does not automatically remove backup files previously created in iCloud Drive.

You can manage or delete these backups through your iCloud Drive storage.

Because the developer does not maintain a separate database containing your household records, there is no developer-held household record database to delete.

## 7. Children's Privacy

House Records is a general-purpose household organization application and is not specifically directed toward children.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Security

House Records uses Apple's platform storage and security technologies to manage locally stored information and iCloud backups.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices, Apple Accounts, and any files they choose to export or share.

No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated to reflect changes in House Records, its features, or its data handling practices.

Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date when appropriate.
